# Executive HR Analytics Dashboard (Power BI) 📊

### Project Overview
Designed and developed an interactive **HR Analytics Dashboard** in **Power BI Desktop** to analyze employee attrition, workforce demographics, and job satisfaction across 1,470 employee records.

### Key KPIs & Metrics Tracked
* **Overall Employees:** 1,470
* **Total Attrition:** 237 (16.12% Attrition Rate)
* **Active Employees:** 1,233
* **Average Employee Age:** 37 Years

### Key Business Insights Discovered
* **Department-Wise Attrition:** **R&D** accounts for the highest attrition share at **56.12% (133 employees)**, followed by **Sales at 38.82% (92)**, and **HR at 5.06% (12)**.
* **Age & Gender Trends:** The **25–34 age group** experiences the highest attrition (**112 employees** — 69 Male, 43 Female), indicating early-career retention challenges.
* **Education Field Impact:** Employees from **Life Sciences** and **Medical** backgrounds show the highest attrition numbers.
* **Job Satisfaction Matrix:** Evaluated satisfaction ratings (1 to 4) across job roles like Laboratory Technician, Sales Executive, and Research Scientist to pinpoint engagement gaps.

### Tools & Power BI Features Used
* **DAX Measures & KPI Cards:** Built dynamic summary cards for real-time workforce tracking.
* **Data Visualizations:** Pie Charts, Stacked Column Charts, Donut Charts, Bar Charts, and Conditional Matrix Tables.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Added top-level filters by **Education Level** (Bachelor's, Master's, Doctoral, etc.) for instant drill-down analysis.
